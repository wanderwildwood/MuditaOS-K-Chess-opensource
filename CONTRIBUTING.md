# Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome. A translation from somebody who speaks the language is
especially welcome: the words Chess+ adds to Mudita's app are in
`library/frontitude/src/main/res/values/strings.xml`, below Mudita's, and show in English until
someone translates them.

Chess+ began as Mudita's Chess for the Kompakt. For the original app, and Mudita's own
process, see [mudita/MuditaOS-K-Chess-opensource](https://github.com/mudita/MuditaOS-K-Chess-opensource).
